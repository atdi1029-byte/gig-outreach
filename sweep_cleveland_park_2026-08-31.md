## NOTES
- Cleveland Park lost its fine dining anchor restaurants (Ardeo, Palena, Ripple) in 2017-2018 wave of closures
- The neighborhood is rebuilding with new concepts: Cork, Little Blackbird, Dakshin
- City Ridge (former Fannie Mae HQ on Wisconsin Ave) is the biggest new development — Acqua Bistecca + Ridge Club
- Woodley Park is compact — commercial strip is essentially one block (2600 Connecticut Ave)
- Cathedral Commons (Wisconsin & Newark) is anchored by Silver + Giant
- Omni Shoreham is the single dominant hotel — massive event space, historic landmark
- No funeral homes, cigar lounges, wineries, or fraternal orgs in these neighborhoods
- No spas with event space found
- FRESHFARM farmers market runs Saturdays Apr-Dec at Connecticut & Newark — books live music
