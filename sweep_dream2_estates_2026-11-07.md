# Dream sweep 2: estates, mansions, embassy-row culture, Hunt Country (Nov 7 2026)

Searched by kind across DC, MD and VA, not town by town: historic mansion and estate wedding guides, embassy-row and Georgetown event venues, hotel lounges with live piano, Hunt Country and Eastern Shore estates, and the Forbes 2026 star awards. WebSearch and WebFetch only, no browser.

Every candidate was checked against the sheet (`dash.json`, 5,306 venues, by name and website domain, whatever their status) and against today's other dream sweeps (hotels, dining, clubs/wine). The sheet already has nearly every famous name in this space (Hillwood, Tudor Place, Dumbarton House, Evermay, Anderson House, Woodrow Wilson House, Oatlands, Morven Park, Whitehall, Woodend, Glenview, Strathmore, Ladew, Evergreen, Inn at Perry Cabin, Tidewater Inn, Salamander, Goodstone, the Red Fox Inn, Antrim 1844 and more), so this list is the ones still missing.

Each venue below has its own official website, fetched, with the name and address read on the page and signs it is active in 2026.
