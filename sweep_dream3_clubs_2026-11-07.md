# Dream sweep 3: private clubs, award-winning wineries, wine bars (Nov 7 2026)

Region-wide search by kind, not town by town, using WebSearch and WebFetch only. No Chrome, no sheet writes.
Sources: the 2026 Virginia Governor's Cup Case and gold medal lists, the 2025 Maryland Governor's Cup results,
Wine Spectator 2025 Maryland award winners, GAYOT / Time Out / Washington Lobbyist wine bar lists, Baltimore
Magazine and Northern Virginia Magazine wine coverage, and the privateiq.golf Maryland and Virginia private club
directories (used as a stand-in for the Platinum Clubs / Golf Digest / Golfweek lists).

Every name was checked against the sheet (dash.json, 5,333 venues, by name and website domain) and against
today's earlier sweeps (sweep_dream_*, sweep_dream2_*) and their Excluded sections. **The sheet already covers
almost every top club and winery in the region.** Every Platinum Club and ranked private club in DC, MD and VA
(Congressional, Chevy Chase, Burning Tree, Columbia, Kenwood, Woodmont, Baltimore CC, Elkridge, Green Spring
Valley, Caves Valley, Robert Trent Jones, Creighton Farms, Westwood, Washington Golf, River Bend, Army Navy...)
is already on it, and so are the city and university clubs (Metropolitan, Cosmos, Sulgrave, University Club,
Arts Club, Army and Navy, Georgetown Club, Maryland Club, Center Club, Hamilton Street, Mount Vernon Club,
Engineers Club). Almost every Governor's Cup winery within about 2 hours, and the best-known wine bars, are on
it too. What's left is short and real.
