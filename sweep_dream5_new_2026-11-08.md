# Dream sweep 5: new and recently recognized upscale venues (2024–2026), DC / MD / VA

Date: 2026-11-08. WebSearch and WebFetch only, no browser. Every name was checked against the sheet
(`dash.json`, 5,366 venues) by name and by website domain, whatever its status.

Sources searched by kind, across the whole region:
- **New-restaurant lists:** Washingtonian's new-restaurant roundups (Nov 2025, Jan 2026, Jun 2026), its
  most-anticipated list for 2026 and its 2025 jazz-brunch guide; Resy's 2025 year-end list and its DC Hit
  Lists (Feb, Jun, Jul, Sep and Oct 2026); Fine Dining Lovers (Apr 2026); Eater DC's 2025 awards (via Secret DC).
- **Best-of and award lists:** Michelin DC new additions (Oct 2025, Sep 2026) and the 2025 stars and Bib list;
  Washingtonian 100 (2026); Northern Virginia Magazine 2025 lists; Baltimore Magazine 2026 and Baltimore
  Fishbowl's 2025 year in review; Forbes 2026; AAA Four Diamond (Jun 2026); Wine Spectator 2025 Maryland winners;
  Virginia and Maryland Governor's Cup.
- **Clubs and venues:** new DC private clubs (Washingtonian, Feb 2025); new hotels in DC, Baltimore, Annapolis,
  Leesburg, Frederick and Talbot County; new event estates; wine-dinner series; jazz brunch.

Most 2024–2026 headliners are already on the sheet. That includes Osteria Mozza, Minetta Tavern, Casamara,
Ox & Olive, Bazaar Meat, Acqua Bistecca, Brasero Atlántico, Maison, Lutèce, Elmina, Isla, Karravaan, The Oak Room,
Bernadette's, The Dabney, Albi, Masseria, Hotel Burg / The Huntōn, Shutters on King, 86 State Circle, 1313 West,
Marmo, Captain's Daughter, Visitation Hotel / Wye Oak Tavern, Ammoora, The Ruxton, Executive Branch, Sixty DC,
Canal House, Hilton Arlington The Key, JW Marriott Reston, Electric Bull and Kent Island Resort. What follows is
what was left.
