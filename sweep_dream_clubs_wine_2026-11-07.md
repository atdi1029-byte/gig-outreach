# Sweep: dream venues, clubs and wine (Nov 7 2026)

Searched by kind across DC, MD and VA, not by town: Michelin DC (Washingtonian's Nov 2025 star list), Forbes 2026, Wine Spectator awards (via En Primeur Club), Golfweek 2026 Maryland private courses, Washingtonian's Sept 2026 wineries list, DC wine bar guides and private club coverage.

Every row was checked against the sheet by name and website domain, and every site below was fetched with WebFetch. The one exception to the fetch is noted on row 28 (Four Seasons Baltimore).

Notes:
- Michelin stars come from the Nov 2025 guide, the latest one found. The 2026 ceremony doesn't appear to have happened yet.
- "Open" means the venue's own site showed current hours, bookings or events and no closure notice. Caves Valley, Fauquier Springs, Quièvremont and Oyster Oyster list no hours on the pages fetched, so for those it only means no closure notice was found.
