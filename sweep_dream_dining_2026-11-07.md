# Dream dining sweep: upscale dining by kind, DC/MD/VA (Nov 7 2026)

Region-wide search by kind: Michelin stars, Washingtonian 100 Very Best 2026, RAMMYS 2026, Forbes 2026, Wine Spectator 2026, private dining guides. Every row was checked against the sheet by name and website domain, and its official site was fetched with name and address on the page.

Michelin stars are from En Primeur Club's list (updated Oct 8 2026), since Michelin's own 2026 page wasn't retrieved. Washingtonian ranks are from En Primeur Club's copy of the list. AAA's Four Diamond PDF couldn't be read, and Baltimore Magazine's best-of and $$$$ pages returned 403, so neither fed this list directly.
