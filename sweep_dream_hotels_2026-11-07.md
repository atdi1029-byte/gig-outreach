# Dream sweep: luxury hotels, boutique hotels, historic inns (DC, MD, VA) - 2026-11-07

Searched by kind across the region: Forbes Travel Guide 2026, Michelin Keys, Washingtonian Travel Awards (May 2026), AAA Diamonds, Conde Nast Traveler Readers' Choice 2026, Travel + Leisure, Historic Hotels of America, Select Registry, Virginia Living, Baltimore magazine.

Every name was checked against the sheet (5,254 venues, by name and website domain, any status). WebSearch and WebFetch only. Drive times from Pasadena, MD are my own estimates, not measured.

Most of the region's top hotels are already on the sheet, so this list is short. Three strong venues are held in Excluded only because their official sites could not be fetched: Sagamore Pendry Baltimore, Airlie and Hotel Washington.

## Excluded

### Strong venues, official site could not be verified (worth a manual look)
- **Sagamore Pendry Baltimore** (1715 Thames St, Fell's Point): Michelin Key, top-tier. Every www.pendry.com/baltimore URL returned 403 and sagamorependry.com returned an empty page. Its Rec Pier Chop House is already on the sheet as needs_review.
- **Airlie** (6809 Airlie Road, Warrenton, VA): Historic Hotels of America estate. airlie.com returned 403 on every URL tried.
- **Hotel Washington** (515 15th St NW, DC): former W Washington with the POV rooftop. thehotelwashington.com returned 403, and I could not confirm 2026 status.
- **Inn at Willow Grove** (Orange, VA): Forbes Recommended 2026, Vintage Restaurant. No street address on any page fetched. About 2h20, past the range.
- **Poplar Springs Inn & Spa** (Casanova/Warrenton, VA): site failed with socket and certificate errors, listings conflict on the address, and 2026 status is unconfirmed.
- **InterContinental Washington DC - The Wharf**: no official site found. Also a brand rather than true luxury, despite ranking second in DC in Conde Nast Traveler 2026 by IHG's own account.
- **Hotel Laura** (Fredericksburg, VA): hotellaura.com redirects to a domain sale page, and no official site found.
- **Inn at Huntingfield Creek** (Rock Hall, MD): only an old Frommer's listing, status unverified.
